# Tug of War Landing Page: Image Prompts

Five image slots. The site currently ships with plain gradient placeholders in `assets/images/`. Generate each image, then **save it with the exact same filename** (JPG, quality 80–85, under ~400 KB) and it drops straight in.

## Shared style (paste at the start of every prompt)

> Bright, premium ed-tech photography. Photorealistic, warm natural window light, shallow depth of field, clean uncluttered background. Indian school-age students in casual school clothes. Colour accents of sky blue and amber gold with small touches of indigo. Soft translucent holographic UI elements floating in the air. Friendly, energetic, joyful mood. Sharp focus on faces. No watermark, no logos, no brand names.

**Negative prompt (paste at the end):** distorted hands, extra fingers, garbled or unreadable text, watermark, logo, cartoon style, dark or moody lighting, cluttered background, adult in frame, violent or aggressive expressions.

**Tip on text:** image models garble letters. Keep any on-screen text to the short numerals given below, and add real text later in Canva or Figma if you need it.

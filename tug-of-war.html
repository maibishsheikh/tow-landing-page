CAMPAIGN STAGES

What Will You Battle?

Each stage focuses on one maths discipline. Play them in order or jump straight to any stage.

⚡
Stage 01

Rookie Turf

Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division warm-ups to build early rope momentum.

  • Addition
  • Subtraction
  • Multiplication facts
  • Division facts
  • Quick mental recall
🎯
Stage 02

Speed Gym

Multi-step expressions where order of operations decides who pulls first.

  • a + b × c
  • a × b − c
  • (a − b) × c
  • a + b ÷ c
  • Mixed × ÷ + practice
🔥
Stage 03

Power Tower

Squares, cubes and clean square roots under match pressure.

  • Squares (a²)
  • Cubes (a³)
  • Square roots (√N)
  • Root plus a number
  • Times-table warm-ups
📐
Stage 04

Shape Colosseum

Geometry and fractions: areas, perimeters, angles and parts of a whole.

  • Area of squares & rectangles
  • Perimeter of rectangles
  • Missing angle in a triangle
  • Halves, quarters & three-quarters
  • 10%, 20%, 25% & 50% of a number
💎
Stage 05

Algebra Champions

Solve for x and spot the rule in number patterns.

  • ax + b = c
  • ax − b = c
  • ax = c and x ÷ b = c
  • Doubling & step patterns
  • Growing-step patterns
👑
Boss Stage

Grand Titan Boss

The championship: every topic from Stages 1–5 mixed into one showdown.

  • All 7 question types
  • Algebra & patterns
  • Powers & roots
  • Geometry & fractions
  • Order of operations

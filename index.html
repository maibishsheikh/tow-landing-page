HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how a match works.

A fast 3D rope battle where every correct maths answer pulls the rope toward your team. Solve quickly and accurately to drag the flag across to your side.

It is designed for Grades 3–8. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard so the numbers fit the player, and jump into any of the six stages.

Each correct answer moves the rope one-fifth of the way. Reach your edge to win instantly. If the 3-minute timer ends first, the team that is ahead wins, and a tie is a draw.

A wrong answer never pushes the rope backwards; it just resets your streak. If the 25-second question clock runs out, you simply get a fresh question.

Yes. Play 1P vs Sparky Bot, or switch to 2-Player and share one screen. Answer with a keyboard or the on-screen number pad.

Every correct answer earns 100 points plus bonus points for streaks. Win a stage to earn 1 to 3 stars: 85% accuracy or more earns 3 stars, and 65% or more earns 2.
